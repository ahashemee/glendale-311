All six districts filed fewer requests in 2024 than in 2023; Cactus fell the most (-22.4%)

January 2020 to July 2026 (79 months) · monthly GlendaleOne request counts by Council District · raw counts, not per resident · every panel uses the same 0 to 500 request scale.

Observations
  • Every district filed fewer requests in 2024 than in 2023. Cactus fell the most, -22.4% (2,258 to 1,753), and Yucca the least, -8.1%. Citywide: -14.6% (16,778 to 14,336).
  • The highest single district-month was Cactus in May 2023, with 408 requests. The highest citywide month was May 2023, with 1,926 requests across the six districts.
  • January to July 2026 against the same months of 2025: Yucca +17.5%, Barrel +15.3%, Cactus +6.4% and Ocotillo +6.4% rose; Cholla -11.2% and Sahuaro -11.6% fell. Citywide: +2.6%.
  • Citywide, November 2024 had 819 requests, the lowest of all 79 months in the window.
