### 1.2 Reshape and conversion rules

- Each report was parsed by section header (bold cell or `ACS Estimate` header) and by the cell indent level in column A, which encodes the label hierarchy. Variable name = `acs_` or `dip_` + section abbreviation + parent labels + label, in snake_case. Parent `Total` rows are left out of child names.
- ACS Population Summary: one column per estimate, plus `_pct` (Percent) and `_moe` (MOE ±) columns. The `Reliability` column is empty in all 6 files and was not carried over.
- Demographic and Income Profile: vintage suffix `_2020` = Census 2020 actual, `_2026` = Esri 2026 estimate, `_2031` = Esri 2031 projection; `_pct` = share. Trend rows are annual growth rates, named `dip_trend_<var>_rate_<area|state|national>`, in percent per year. There is no MOE in this report.
- **All percentages and rates are on a 0-100 scale** (Excel stores them as fractions, e.g. 0.292 → 29.2; the `100.0%` text cells → 100).
- Strings were stripped of commas, $, %, +/-, ± and footnote markers; placeholders (N, (X), -, **, blank) became empty cells. Placeholders hit: dip:hhinc: 36 (the `-` cells are the Percent column of Median/Average/Per Capita Income).
- Columns dropped because they were empty for all 6 districts: ACS ['acs_total_population_pct', 'acs_total_households_pct', 'acs_total_housing_units_pct', 'acs_hh_poverty_poverty_index_pct', 'acs_hh_poverty_poverty_index_moe', 'acs_commute_time_average_travel_time_to_work_in_minutes', 'acs_commute_time_average_travel_time_to_work_in_minutes_pct', 'acs_commute_time_average_travel_time_to_work_in_minutes_moe', 'acs_computer_population_65plus_in_households', 'acs_computer_population_65plus_in_households_pct', 'acs_computer_population_65plus_in_households_moe']; DIP ['dip_hhinc_median_household_income_2026_pct', 'dip_hhinc_median_household_income_2031_pct', 'dip_hhinc_average_household_income_2026_pct', 'dip_hhinc_average_household_income_2031_pct', 'dip_hhinc_per_capita_income_2026_pct', 'dip_hhinc_per_capita_income_2031_pct']. `Average Travel Time to Work` has no value in any file; `Population 65+ in Households` has no estimate (its pct/MOE are also blank).
